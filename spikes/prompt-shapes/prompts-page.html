The page above is built to these three. Everything else about it stays open until it is read in a browser.

1. Where prompts live

A page of its own in the stage strip, which is what the path down the left side is for. Other pages carry a link into it where that helps: the build page from its prompt rules, and any step in the drawing from its own card.

A page of its ownRuled 3 September. Reads in one sitting and stays readable at forty steps.
A section on the build pageSet aside. A long project made a very long page.

2. What opens first

The page opens on one real run, with the traveller's own words in place. The text as written is one click away and stays available on every step.

As sentRuled 3 September. A cut sentence or a mangled list only shows itself in the filled text.
As writtenSet aside as the opening state, kept as the toggle.

3. What a comment attaches to

All three: a value, a whole prompt, and any words you select. A selection thread keeps the words and the run they came from, so it still reads correctly after the text changes.

All threeRuled 3 September. Selecting words is how you comment on wording, and wording is most of what there is to say.
