# Expense claims

## What this is

An agent that reads an expense claim as it was written, checks it against the expense policy,
and says approve, reject or escalate, with the reason it gives a person.

## Who it is for

The person in finance who processes claims. They know the policy and they do not want to read
two hundred claims a month to find the six that break it.

## What it works on

A claim as the employee submitted it: free text, a date, an amount, and whatever proof was
attached. The policy is the company's own, with a limit per category.

## Where this is going and where it is not

It decides the claims the policy settles and hands the rest to a person. It never pays
anything, never edits a claim, and never talks to the employee who submitted it.

## What is still open

Whether it should read the receipt image as well as the text of the claim.
